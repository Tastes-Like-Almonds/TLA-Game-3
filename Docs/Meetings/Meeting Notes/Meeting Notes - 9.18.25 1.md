## Story Outline
___
Current plot as of today: A brave adventurer (backstory TBD) embarks upon a selfless journey to save a princess. He travels about the dangers of the world as his companion and guide aids him through his quest. At the middle of the game, there will be an encounter with the big bad, and once again at the end. Once arriving at the big bad's castle, he is somewhat easily defeated by the hero. The hero saves the princess and begins to leave the castle, but just as they make it out the guide stops them. 

This part isn't too fleshed out but basically the princess is corrupted by an evil artifact and the companion wants to steal it for power. Final fight ensues after.
